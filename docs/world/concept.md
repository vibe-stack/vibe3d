# World concept — Axiom Relay

## One-sentence promise

**Axiom Relay is a reclaimed planetary infrastructure network where elegant
phase-era civic technology has been forced to coexist with patched industrial
machinery, military containment, and fast-growing ecologies.**

The kit should feel authored by many operators over decades, but governed by
one underlying engineering logic: a clean structural chassis, a dark service
layer, one readable power signal, and visible access to whatever keeps the
machine alive.

## The world in practical terms

The Axiom Relay was a chain of climate, transit, energy, research, and logistics
facilities built around long-range phase gates. A system-wide failure stranded
settlements and disrupted ownership. Civic districts, military installations,
research campuses, industrial yards, frontier settlements, and wild zones now
share the same abandoned backbone.

That premise explains why the same things recur everywhere:

- structural panels use a shared 1 m engineering grid;
- power, cooling, data, and access services remain exposed;
- old clean shells are repaired with darker field parts;
- public-facing spaces use stronger signage and deliberate color accents;
- dangerous or biological spaces add temporary containment layers;
- destruction reveals the same layered construction instead of random rubble.

## Five place families

These are not separate art styles. They are material and dressing emphases on
the same chassis.

| Place family | What changes | What must remain shared |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frontier / military | heavier barriers, visible repairs, low saturation, practical cover | grid, service logic, edge treatment, signal colors |
| Industrial / thermal | heat staining, pipes, tanks, gantries, dense machinery | dark service layer, modular interfaces, readable maintenance access |
| Clean civic / scientific | lighter shells, glass, gardens, precise repeating geometry | graphite undersides, restrained emissive use, structural hierarchy |
| Dense urban / night | signage, retail clutter, layered facades, wet surfaces, social traces | scale grid, standardized utilities, controlled neon roles |
| Ecological / lunar / disaster | rock, growth, ice, dust, quarantine, storm damage | manufactured objects still reveal the Axiom chassis beneath the dressing |

## Faction and ownership language

Use ownership as a subtle material/signage layer, never as a new shape language.
The inventory may use legacy labels such as IMC, Hammond, ECHO, or Games for
production reference. New geometry should use the neutral Axiom Relay mark set
from [`color-system.md`](color-system.md) and the logo placeholders in the
graphics package; do not reproduce third-party logos or exact branded assets.

| Internal owner | Visual behavior | Typical asset use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Axiom Civic | warm-white shell, cobalt wayfinding, glass, planted negative space | Olympus, universities, clinics, luxury districts |
| Axiom Works | graphite service backs, coated alloy, amber status lamps, dense pipe logic | factories, docks, power stations, thermal sites |
| Axiom Defense | low-gloss dark shell, pale panels, high-visibility hazard marks, robust fasteners | armories, bunkers, checkpoints, watchtowers |
| Axiom Field | prefabricated panels, green monitoring marks, weather gear, temporary braces | ECHO-style camps, wildlife zones, storm infrastructure |
| Local / reclaimed | mixed shells, repainted panels, improvised labels, civilian color touches | Old Town, markets, settlements, damaged POIs |

## Tone and restraint

The target is high-contrast, readable, athletic science fiction: bold silhouettes,
clean landmarks, tactile wear, and energized accents. It is not grimy realism,
pure cyberpunk, military cosplay, or sterile white futurism. Keep the visual
temperature optimistic enough for play, even when the story says a place is
damaged.

Every asset should answer three questions at a glance:

1. What is it for?
2. Who maintains or owns it?
3. What state is it in right now?
