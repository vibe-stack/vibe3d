# Map identity and dressing rules

The maps are distinct chapters of one infrastructure network. Each map gets a
dominant material/atmosphere mix and a limited signal palette; it does not get a
new geometry language. Use this document when placing reusable assets or
reviewing a hero assembly.

## Kings Canyon — frontier military and ecological disaster

**Read:** old defense infrastructure, hard cliffs, salvage, containment, and
facilities that have outlived their operators.

- Base neutrals: `INK-900`, `GRAPHITE-800`, `SHELL-200`, `RUST-500`, `DUST-300`.
- Signals: `AMBER-400` for defense/service, `FIELD-500` for field monitoring,
  `RED-500` for breach or disaster.
- Geometry: heavy IMC-style prefabs, bunker cuts, retaining walls, cages,
  bridges, exposed utility runs, and repaired steel.
- Terrain: dry mineral, red oxide, cliff strata, scrub, and ecological traces
  that enter through cracks and abandoned compounds.
- Composition: let one military silhouette dominate; keep salvage and wildlife
  as readable secondary stories rather than noise.

Key visual chapters include Airbase, Artillery Battery, ARES Capacitor, Crash
Site, Spotted Lake, Creature Containment, Crypto's Map Room, Repulsor, Singh
Labs, Caustic Treatment, Octane's Gauntlet, Relic, Runoff, Offshore Rig,
Swamps, Cage, The Pit, Watchtowers, and Bunker Pass.

## World's Edge — volcanic industry and frozen extraction

**Read:** industrial ambition forced to operate across lava, ice, geothermal
weather, and dense worker infrastructure.

- Base neutrals: `INK-950`, `GRAPHITE-800`, `RUST-500`, `DUST-300`, `ICE-300`.
- Signals: `ORANGE-500` for heat, `AMBER-400` for industrial caution,
  `CYAN-400` for cooling/data, `RED-500` for failure.
- Geometry: large turbines, gantries, cooling towers, siphons, rails, gondolas,
  extraction legs, prefabricated worker buildings, and heavily serviced roofs.
- Terrain: black volcanic mineral, lava crust, wind-scoured concrete, frost,
  snow accumulation, and heat-transition stains.
- Composition: thermal landmarks need a strong vertical or radial read; frozen
  dressing should frame machinery and never wash the entire map white.

Key visual chapters include Planet Harvester, Climatizer, Lava Siphon, Skyhook,
The Epicenter, Thermal Station, The Geyser, Monument, Big Maude, Mirage à
Trois, Launch Site, Countdown, Stacks, Fragment, Landslide, Lava Fissure, The
Dome, The Tree, Staging, Survey Camp, and the historical Train/Sorting Factory
library.

## Olympus — clean civic utopia and scientific megastructure

**Read:** a prosperous, carefully maintained public future with quiet technical
power underneath its gardens, glass, and white shells.

- Base neutrals: `SHELL-050`, `SHELL-200`, `INK-900`, `GRAPHITE-800`.
- Signals: `COBALT-500` for civic identity, `CYAN-400` for active transit/data,
  `VIOLET-500` for phase systems, `LIME-400` for cultivation.
- Geometry: precise slabs, circular platforms, glass bridges, radial devices,
  clean service galleries, landscaped voids, and controlled repeating trim.
- Terrain: sky, polished mineral, planted beds, water, and limited grime at
  service edges. Dirt belongs at contact points and maintenance zones.
- Composition: preserve generous negative space and long sightlines; use one
  high-value signal per facade plane rather than a neon cluster.

Key visual chapters include Rift, Phase Runner, Turbine, Hammond Labs, Bonsai
Plaza, Oasis, Elysium, Carrier, Grow Towers, Solar Array, Hydroponics, Icarus,
Fight Night, Clinic, Phase Driver, Somers University, Gravity Engine,
Stabilizer, and Dockyard.

## Storm Point — tropical brutalism and weather infrastructure

**Read:** abandoned or active field infrastructure under salt, rain, storms,
wildlife pressure, and coastal exposure.

- Base neutrals: `GRAPHITE-800`, `SHELL-200`, `MAT-11` concrete tones, `FIELD-500`.
- Signals: `FIELD-500` for environmental operations, `LIME-400` for ecology,
  `AMBER-400` for storm/service, `RED-500` for strike or breach.
- Geometry: tall turbines, storm towers, bunkers, raised pads, flood walls,
  field camps, bridges, cages, and exposed anchors.
- Terrain: wet dark stone, tropical growth, salt staining, cloud shadow, pools,
  coastal debris, and lightning scars.
- Composition: use vertical weather structures as skyline anchors; let plants,
  water, and damage penetrate the kit at believable contact points.

Key visual chapters include Barometer, Storm Catcher, Command Center, The Wall,
Lightning Rod, The Mill, Checkpoint, Launch Pad, Wattson's Pylon, CETO Station,
ZEUS Station, ECHO HQ, Devastated Coast, Downed Beast, Cenote Cave, Forbidden
Zone, IMC Armories, North Pad, Cascade Falls, and Coastal Camp.

## Broken Moon — lunar colony and terraforming megatech

**Read:** engineered habitat under low gravity, large terraforming systems,
African-futurist civic warmth, dust, fracture, and quarantine.

- Base neutrals: `INK-950`, `SHELL-200`, `GRAPHITE-800`, `DUST-300`, `ICE-300`.
- Signals: `COBALT-500` for colony wayfinding, `VIOLET-500` for phase/stasis,
  `LIME-400` for cultivation, `RED-500` for quarantine.
- Geometry: domes, pods, radial machines, suspended bridges, huge foundations,
  covered transit loops, service tunnels, and visible life-support logic.
- Terrain: lunar dust, fractured rock, hard-edged quarry cuts, sparse growth,
  contaminated biological material, and sharp light/shadow separation.
- Composition: make the horizon and foundation scale legible; use curved habitat
  modules and radial machines as deliberate counterpoints to the grid.

Key visual chapters include Terraformer, Stasis Array, The Core, Atmostation,
Production Yard, Foundry, Bionomics, Cultivation, Eternal Gardens, The Divide,
Alpha Base, Breaker Wharf, Quarantine Zone, Space Port, Cliff Side, Solar Pods,
Underpass, Experimental Labs, Hazmat Tunnel, and Dry Gulch.

## E-District — dense urban life, commerce, and infrastructure

**Read:** a socially layered city where luxury, work, nightlife, civic tension,
water, and utilities occupy the same vertical block.

- Base neutrals: `INK-950`, `INK-900`, `GRAPHITE-800`, `SHELL-200`.
- Signals: `MAGENTA-400` for nightlife/commerce, `CYAN-400` for data/transit,
  `LIME-400` for planted civic life, `AMBER-400` for work zones, `RED-500`
  for unrest or failure.
- Geometry: narrow streets, stacked balconies, canopy layers, signs, wires,
  rooftop service, canals, malls, towers, storefront rhythm, and utility clutter.
- Terrain: wet asphalt, concrete, glass, planted beds, graffiti, rubble, and
  localized stains. Do not smear dirt uniformly across the district.
- Composition: alternate quiet and loud blocks; give the player a readable
  route through signage cadence, light direction, and strong vertical landmarks.

Key visual chapters include Energy Bank, The Lotus, Shipyard Arcade, Boardwalk,
City Hall, Resort, Electro Dam, Galleria, Heights, Humbert Labs, Old Town,
Stadium, Blossom Drive, Draft Point, Neon Square, Street Market, and Viaduct.

## Cross-map rule

When a reusable prop moves between maps, keep its chassis, proportions, socket
layout, and functional logic stable. Localize it through `MAT-17` graphics,
wear masks, terrain contact, limited shell recolor, lighting, and surrounding
dressing. A map variant that needs a new silhouette is a new asset or a hero
component, not an untracked "color variant."
